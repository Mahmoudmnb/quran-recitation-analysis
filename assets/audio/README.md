# Audio assets policy

No Quran recitation audio is distributed here. The project's dataset is excluded due to ownership, licensing, and redistribution considerations.

Add public sample audio only when redistribution rights are clear and documented. Sample audio must not contain restricted or copyrighted content without permission from the relevant rights holders. Access to a recording does not itself establish permission to redistribute it.

Do not upload private dataset excerpts or private recordings. Review any future asset's provenance and permission terms before including it. The repository license does not grant rights to third-party audio or Quran recitation recordings.
